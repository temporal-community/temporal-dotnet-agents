## Expense Report Instructions

To file an expense report:
1. Gather all receipts dated within the current quarter.
2. Complete form EX-100 with merchant name, amount, and business purpose.
3. Attach digital scans (PDF or JPG) for any amount over $25.
4. Submit via the HR portal by the 5th of the following month.
5. Approval is required from your direct manager for amounts over $500.

Reimbursement is processed within 10 business days of approval.
